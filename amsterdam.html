Day 2 · 10/25（日）
WINDMILL DAY · SUN OCT 25
🌷 風車村一日遊
Zaanse Schans Windmill Village Day Trip
🏆 必去！Must Visit
🇳🇱
贊瑟斯漢斯風車村
Zaanse Schans · FREE Village Entry
荷蘭最經典的風車景觀！保存了 18-19 世紀的荷蘭傳統風車、木屋、起司工坊與木鞋工廠。整個村莊免費進入，只有個別風車內部或博物館需要門票。The Netherlands' most iconic windmill landscape! Preserved 18-19th century Dutch windmills, wooden houses, cheese farms & clog workshops. Village entry is FREE — only individual windmill interiors & museums charge admission.

上午：起司工坊 & 木鞋工廠
Morning: Cheese Farm & Clog Workshop
參觀 Catharina Hoeve 起司農場（免費參觀+試吃！）看師傅現場示範手工製作荷蘭傳統起司。再到 木鞋工廠 Kooijman（免費）看荷蘭人如何用一塊木頭雕出一雙木鞋。Visit Catharina Hoeve cheese farm (free entry + tasting!) Watch cheese made by hand. Then Kooijman Clog Workshop (free) — see how wooden clogs are carved from a single block of wood.
💨
下午：風車巡禮 & 河畔漫步
Afternoon: Windmill Walk & Riverside
沿著贊河 (Zaan River) 散步，一排排的風車矗立在綠色草地上，這就是明信片上的荷蘭！每座風車各有功能：磨香料、鋸木頭、榨油。進入風車參觀門票約 €5/座，選 1-2 座體驗即可。Walk along the Zaan River with windmills lining the green fields — this IS the Netherlands on postcards! Each mill has a different function: spice grinding, sawing wood, oil pressing. Entry ~€5/mill — visit 1-2 for the experience.
